The problem

The shelves are always empty.

Every customer-education platform on the market sells you a library and wishes you luck. The tool takes an afternoon to set up. The content takes a quarter nobody has, and it lands on whoever has the least leverage to refuse.

  • It's nobody's job.

    Onboarding content sits between product, support and customer success. It gets written once during a push, and then nobody owns keeping it true.

  • It loses to the customer in front of you.

    Content is always the thing that can slip. A live escalation never is. So it slips, every week, permanently.

  • The first version is the cheap part.

    Building the library once is a project. Keeping it accurate is a job — and the job is the part nobody costs out.
